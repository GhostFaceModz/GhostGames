About webRcade

Open-source retro arcade with free homebrew games for NES, SNES, Genesis, GBA and more — or load your own ROMs.

Controls

Arrow keysD-padEnterStart / confirmZ / XFace buttonsEscBack / menu

Controllers are supported.

Tips

  • Opens in a new tab — webRcade needs cross-origin isolation that our player can't provide.
  • Browse free homebrew feeds, or load ROMs you own from your device.
  • Works on PC and mobile browsers.

Game not loading?

  • Reload the game: a fresh start fixes most stuck loading screens.
  • Chrome or Edge on PC work best. Turn off ad blockers and privacy shields for this site.
  • Click inside the game to give it keyboard and mouse focus. Press Esc to release the mouse.
