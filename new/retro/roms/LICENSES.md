# GhostGames Retro: bundled homebrew ROM licences

Only free, open-source homebrew is bundled here. No commercial or copyrighted ROMs are hosted.
The "Retro Player" lets visitors load ROMs they own from their own device; those files are opened locally and never uploaded.
